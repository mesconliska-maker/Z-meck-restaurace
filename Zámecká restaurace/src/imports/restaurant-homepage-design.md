Design a premium modern homepage for a Czech restaurant called "Zámecká restaurace Horšovský Týn".

The restaurant is located in the historic town center near a castle and offers traditional Czech and international cuisine. The goal is to transform an outdated website into a modern, elegant and highly attractive restaurant presentation that motivates visitors to personally visit the restaurant or make a reservation.
